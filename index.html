GPR-Based Probabilistic Surrogate Model for Fatigue Safety Factor Prediction

Built a Gaussian Process Regression surrogate model to generate probabilistic predictions of fatigue safety factors from five geometric parameters of a stepped shaft — eliminating the need for repeated ANSYS FEA simulations during design exploration.

R² = 0.9905 Test Accuracy
RMSE 0.187 On 314 ANSYS Points
CV R² = 0.971 5-Fold Cross-Validation
